What is a Tour Guide License in India?

A Tour Guide License is a mandatory certification issued by the Ministry of Tourism, Government of India, allowing individuals to legally conduct tours. It ensures guides meet national standards for knowledge, safety, and hospitality. Without this license, operating as a professional guide is illegal and risks fines or bans.

Why Is a Valid Tour Guide License Essential for Travelers?

Travelers choose licensed guides for safety, accuracy, and authentic cultural insights. A valid license guarantees the guide has passed rigorous training in history, geography, and emergency protocols. This ensures a secure, informative, and enjoyable experience for every tourist visiting India.
