Things To Do

Machu Picchu has temples, residences, platforms, and roads, and most of these are included in the tourist route with a guide.

Some places, like the Intipunku and Inca Bridge, require optional routes to enter, while others, like the Temple of the Moon, require a different ticket. The most popular way to get to Machu Picchu is by hiking the 4-day Inca Trail, which takes hikers through the Andes, past lesser-known Inca sites, and ends at the Sun Gate. There's also a 2-day Inca Trail for those who want a shorter or less challenging hike.

The trail includes steep stone staircases and narrow paths, and hikers feel a sense of accomplishment when they reach the Sun Gate.

What is Machu Picchu and why is it a must-visit destination?

Machu Picchu is an ancient Incan citadel set high in the Andes Mountains of Peru. It is a UNESCO World Heritage site and one of the New Seven Wonders of the World. Visitors are drawn to its stunning architecture, breathtaking mountain views, and deep historical significance. This site offers a unique blend of adventure, culture, and natural beauty that captivates travelers from around the globe.

What should you pack for a comfortable Machu Picchu adventure?

For a comfortable Machu Picchu adventure, pack lightweight, breathable clothing suitable for varying weather conditions. Comfortable hiking shoes are essential for navigating the stone pathways and trails. Bring a small daypack with water, snacks, sunscreen, and a rain jacket. A camera or smartphone will help you capture the stunning landscapes. These essentials will ensure you stay prepared and enjoy your journey to the top.
