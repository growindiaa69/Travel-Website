Frequently Asked Questions About Curated Travel

How do I book my travel arrangements through your website?

To book your travel arrangements, navigate to the 'Book Now' section on the Tourguide homepage. Select your desired destination, choose your preferred dates, and review the available tour packages. Click 'Confirm Booking' to proceed to the secure payment gateway. You will receive an instant email confirmation with your itinerary details and a unique booking reference number for your records.

What are the benefits of booking with your website?

Tourguide offers a diverse range of curated travel experiences, including guided cultural heritage tours, adventure expeditions, and luxury leisure packages. Each tour is designed to provide an authentic immersion into local traditions, ensuring travelers enjoy memorable and well-organized journeys tailored to their interests and comfort levels.

What measures are in place to ensure the safety of my personal information?

We safeguard all traveler data using 256-bit SSL encryption, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways, and strict ISO-27001 data protection protocols. Your booking credentials and contact records are never sold, rented, or shared with unverified third parties under any circumstances.

Can I make changes to my booking or cancel it if needed?

Yes, you can modify or cancel your curated travel booking conveniently online. Cancellations made 24 to 48 hours prior to your scheduled trip receive a 100% full refund with $0 cancellation penalty. Date changes requested at least 7 days in advance are processed free of charge by our 24/7 customer care team.
